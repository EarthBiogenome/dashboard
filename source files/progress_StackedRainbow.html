Eukaryotic assemblies in INSDC databases and the fraction contributed by EBP

What is shown: Each arc represents one taxonomic rank (species, genus, family, order, class, phylum). The full arc (light grey background) is the total number of eukaryotic taxa at that rank in NCBI taxonomy. The inner colored segment shows taxa with any assembly in INSDC; the darker overlapping segment shows taxa covered by at least one EBP-affiliated assembly (NCBI BioProject PRJNA533106).

What the numbers mean: Coverage is defined as the number of taxa that have at least one submitted assembly, regardless of assembly quality or completeness. EBP-affiliated assemblies span a range of quality levels — not all have reached chromosomal level, and chromosomal-level assemblies in INSDC do not necessarily meet EBP quality standards. The chart therefore reflects taxonomic breadth of coverage, not assembly quality.

Data source: Genomes on a Tree (GoaT). Used with permission.
